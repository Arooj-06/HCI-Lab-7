02 / A little about me

Less assumption.
More understanding.

I’m Alex, a curious HCI student learning to turn complicated problems into clear, useful experiences.

I like the part where research challenges an idea, a messy sketch starts to make sense, and a prototype opens up a better conversation. My work connects a love of visual craft with a growing understanding of how people think and behave.

Away from the screen: collecting independent magazines, taking long photo walks, and noticing the little things most people walk past.

Still learning. Always asking better questions.

04 / How I work

A process, not a straight line.

Room to go back.

Room to get better.

01

Listen

Start with people. Observe, ask questions and understand the context before designing a solution.

02

Make sense

Connect the dots. Turn research into clear needs, opportunities and a focused problem to solve.

03

Make it real

Think on paper, then in prototypes. Explore alternatives and make the important interactions tangible.

04

Learn & refine

Put ideas in front of people. Listen to what works, question what doesn’t, and make it better.
